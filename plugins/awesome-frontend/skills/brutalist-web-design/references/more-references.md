# More Brutalist References (measured)

Three more sites across the raw→bold spectrum, captured live (tokens +
screenshots). Anchor: gumroad.com (neo-brutalist). The family spans *pure raw*
(exposed defaults) → *hard luxury-brutalist* → *loud flat-color*.

## Brutalist Websites — `brutalistwebsites.com` (pure raw end) · `brutalistwebsites.png`
- Tokens: bg `#EEEEEE`, **default link blue `#0000EE` text**, **monospace**,
  `radius: 0`, H1 ~13px.
- Read: the canonical directory of brutalist sites — and itself textbook raw
  brutalism: system/monospace type, default-blue underlined links, no styling,
  exposed structure. The reference for "remove all polish; let the raw HTML show."
  Use it as a gallery to browse dozens more examples.

## Balenciaga — `balenciaga.com` (hard luxury-brutalist) · `balenciaga.png`
- Tokens: bg `#FFFFFF`, text `#000`, accent **red `#EB1500`**; font **Helvetica
  Neue**; `radius: 4px`; H1 ~14px (tiny, tracked).
- Read: high-fashion brutalism — stark black Helvetica on white, tiny uppercase
  tracked nav, hard edges, blunt full-bleed imagery, near-zero ornament. Proof
  brutalism can read *expensive* when it's this disciplined: it's the cold,
  industrial cousin of luxury. (Helvetica + hard edges + starkness.)

## Cash App — `cash.app` (loud flat-color end) · `cashapp.png`
- Tokens: dominant **electric green** canvas; black **Cash Sans** bold grotesque;
  black/white pill buttons; `radius: ~20px`; H1 ~56px.
- Read: a single saturated flat color field + big bold grotesque + chunky pills.
  This is the *bold-flat* cousin of brutalism — loud, un-corporate, confident flat
  color — without the black borders/offset shadows. Reach for it when you want
  brutalist energy but friendlier and rounder.

## The transferable spread
Decide how raw: **pure** (system/mono, default links, radius 0, exposed),
**hard** (Helvetica, stark black/white, hard edges, tiny tracked type), or **bold
flat** (one loud saturated color, big bold grotesque, chunky shapes). All refuse
gloss; they differ in how much they keep vs. strip.

# More Editorial References (measured)

Three more editorial sites, captured live (tokens + screenshots). Together they
show that "editorial" is a **spectrum** — from loud-bold to elegant-serif to
clean-grid — not one look. Anchor: heyhoncho.com (see case study).

## Gretel — `gretelny.com` (bold end) · `gretel.png`
- Tokens: bg `#FFFFFF`, text `#1D1D1D`, accent black; font **Akkurat** (a precise
  Swiss grotesque); `radius: 0`; H1 ~72px.
- Read: a design studio site that's editorial via **scale and restraint** — big
  confident grotesque statements, hard edges, lots of white, work presented like
  a magazine. Proof that editorial doesn't require a serif; a strong grotesque set
  large + disciplined space reads editorial too.

## COLLINS — `wearecollins.com` (elegant-serif end) · `collins.png`
- Tokens: bg `#F8F8F7` (warm off-white), text `#140700`, accent **orange `#FF7600`**
  (used in imagery, not chrome); fonts **Portrait Text** (high-contrast serif) +
  **Graphik** (grotesque); `radius: 5px`; H1 ~72px.
- Read: a single enormous serif statement ("Rewrite your worth.") centered in a
  vast empty field, tiny wordmark, understated award row, then full-bleed amber
  imagery. The serif + space = the refined, *literary* edge of editorial (borders
  on luxury). Pairs a contrast serif for statements with a grotesque for labels.

## BUCK — `buck.co` (clean-grid end) · `buck.png`
- Tokens: bg `#E6E6E6` (light gray), text `#000`, accent blue `#196EFA`; font
  **Mabry** (geometric-ish grotesque); `radius: 0`; H1 ~28px (small).
- Read: a creative/animation company; a short intro line + a **two-column grid of
  work thumbnails** (often video). Editorial as a *clean contents grid* — modest
  type, work-forward, rhythmic. The closest of the three to heyhoncho's index.

## The transferable spread
Editorial isn't a single recipe — it's **commitment + space + a strong type
voice**, which can be bold (Gretel), serif-elegant (Collins), or grid-clean
(Buck). Pick the point on the spectrum that fits the brand, then commit.
